# Community code of conduct

Roppo welcomes contributors and users with different backgrounds, experience levels, and languages. This policy applies to project issues, pull requests, discussions, and other spaces maintained by the project.

## Working together

Be respectful and specific. Critique ideas and code rather than people. Explain evidence, acknowledge uncertainty, accept corrections, and give others room to learn. Disagreement is welcome when it helps solve the problem.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory or sexualized abuse, impersonation, deliberate disruption, spam, and publishing another person's private information are not acceptable. Do not publish credentials or private conversations without permission.

## Moderation and reporting

For a public moderation concern, flag the relevant content to repository administrators using GitHub's report controls where available. For abuse requiring private handling, use [GitHub's abuse reporting channel](https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse); do not post private details in an issue. Vulnerability reports use the separate [security policy](SECURITY.md).

Maintainers may edit or remove content, lock discussions, warn participants, or restrict participation in proportion to the behavior and its impact. Repeated or severe violations may result in a ban. Decisions should be based on observable conduct and applied consistently, including to maintainers. A participant may request reconsideration through the moderation thread or GitHub's available reporting process without repeating the harmful content.
